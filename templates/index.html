STAY ONE STEP AHEAD

Everyday Safety Tips

Small habits can prevent serious digital risks.

01

Pause on urgency

Scammers often pressure people to act quickly. Take time to verify.

02

Check the domain

Look closely for misspellings and unexpected subdomains.

03

Protect private codes

Never share passwords, PINs, or OTPs with callers or messages.

04

Verify at the source

Contact banks, sellers, and certificate issuers using official channels.
